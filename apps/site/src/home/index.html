09 Questions

Questions security teams ask first

Is this legal?

VoidAccess searches publicly reachable sources: Tor search engines, code hosts and public threat feeds. It doesn't log into closed forums or buy data. Every account agrees to our Acceptable Use Policy, which limits use to security research and authorised investigation.

Will it show me illegal content?

Safety filters block prohibited queries, links and page content before anything reaches you, and results never link to dark-web pages. Found addresses are shown as text you can copy, not links.

What happens to my queries?

Your investigations are kept while your account is open and deleted when you delete it. Queries and collected text go to our AI providers to refine the search and write the summary, never with your account details. Findings go into an anonymous, dated archive that never contains your identity or your query text. Read the privacy policy.

Who is it not for?

Stalking, doxxing, harassment, or anyone looking for illegal content or services. We close accounts that try.

Can I self-host instead?

Yes. pip install voidaccess, MIT licence, free forever.
